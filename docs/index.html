JobAgent

Upload your CV and get Indeed vacancies ranked by how well they fit you.

  1. Reads your PDF CV and turns it into structured data.
  2. Comes up with search terms that fit your profile.
  3. Searches Indeed for jobs near you.
  4. Pulls the key facts out of each vacancy: salary, requirements, remote work.
  5. Scores every job against your CV and ranks them from best to worst match.

Run it locally

uv sync
uv run streamlit run src/job_search/app.py
